The concept

  1. Afterimages. Every chain finished on time leaves a frozen afterimage (max 3), typed by its last key: R thrust ➤, E sweep ◠, Q rising cut ⤴. They fire in creation order at the burst.
  2. Patterns. 3 different = Cross-cut (×3.0). 3 the same = Focused triple (×2.4, but +45 tension a chain instead of +34).
  3. Sheath tension. Clean chains fill it; full = the draw crits (×1.6). A dropped chain only slows the build, it never wipes it.
  4. 20 s cycle. ~14 s build (about 3 chains + basics on the 1 s GCD), then the draw cashes the afterimages + tension.
  5. One Perfect Cut. Every 3rd burst (60 s) links every afterimage of the minute into one line cut (×1.8 of their sum).
  6. Opener. Hold the draw before the pull; releasing on the pull places the first afterimage (+25 tension).
Real game: the Samurai, its chains (Counter Sheathe, Petal Rain, Wind Step, Moment of Death) and the boss are the live game engine. Concept: the afterimages, tension, draw and every number marked CONCEPT are placeholders for discussion. No balance changed.
