Samurai: Delayed Cuts cycle CONCEPT

A scripted mock-up, not the game: 60 s rotation vs Grimtusk. All numbers are placeholders.

Speed Route
  1. Banking: every boss chain finisher banks Delayed Cuts; heavier chains bank more.
  2. Cash-out: the Special chain (Void Draw) releases the bank in waves; the finisher releases the rest.
  3. Resheathe: the Special finisher opens a 6 s window where chains bank double.
  4. Cycle: Special ~20 s: about 14 s banking, cash-out, Resheathe.
  5. Opener: a held pre-pull draw banks 3; the pull opens with the Special, then the heaviest chains.
  6. One Perfect Cut inside Resheathe releases the whole bank ×2; lines up with every 3rd Special (~60 s).
